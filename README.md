# FedBridge — CMMC Intelligence for Defense Contractors

Weekly compliance briefs that surface only the regulation changes relevant to your specific contracts.

**Live page:** [zmsol.github.io/fedbridge](https://zmsol.github.io/fedbridge)

## What it does

Small defense contractors can't afford a full-time compliance officer, but missing one CMMC rule change can disqualify them from every bid in the pipeline. FedBridge tracks DFARS, CMMC, and NIST changes weekly and delivers a plain-English brief every Monday.

- Tracks federal register updates across CMMC, DFARS, and NIST frameworks
- Filters by contract type and certification level
- Plain-English summaries — no regulatory jargon
- Delivered Monday morning via email

## Distribution

Content is distributed via email subscription. The landing page at [zmsol.github.io/fedbridge](https://zmsol.github.io/fedbridge) explains the service and captures leads.
