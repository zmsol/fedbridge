FedBridge Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 30, 2026

FedBridge Intelligence ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of the defense contracting professionals and organizations who subscribe to our intelligence briefs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary for delivering our subscription service and improving our content:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, title, company, and email address.
  • Subscription Information: Details regarding your current subscription status.
  • Usage Data: Non-identifiable data related to how you interact with our email briefs to help us optimize content delivery.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: To send the FedBridge Weekly Intelligence Brief to your email.
  • Communication: To send renewal notices, billing updates, and service-related announcements.
  • Improvement: To analyze usage patterns to improve the quality of the intelligence provided.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only with service providers who perform services for us, or if required to do so by law.

4. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. No security measures are perfect, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard your data.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: privacy@fedbridge.io
