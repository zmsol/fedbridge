# CMMC Levels Explained: A Guide for Small Defense Contractors

> **The short version:** CMMC 2.0 has three levels. Most small contractors need Level 1 (self-assessment) or Level 2 (certified assessment). Here's exactly what each costs, requires, and how to tell which you need.

---

## What Is CMMC?

The Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) is the Department of Defense's standard for protecting controlled unclassified information (CUI). If you hold a DoD contract, you must be certified at the appropriate level.

**There is no opt-out.** If you want to bid on or renew DoD contracts, CMMC compliance is mandatory.

---

## The Three Levels at a Glance

| Level | Name | Assessment Type | Who Needs It |
|-------|------|----------------|--------------|
| **Level 1** | Foundational | Self-assessment | Contractors handling Federal Contract Information (FCI) only |
| **Level 2** | Advanced | Certified assessment (C3PAO) or self-assessment | Contractors handling Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) |
| **Level 3** | Expert | Government-led assessment | Contractors handling the highest-risk CUI programs |

---

## Level 1 — Foundational (Self-Assessment)

**If you handle FCI only.** FCI is information not intended for public release — think basic contract data, payment information, delivery schedules.

- **Requirements:** 17 practices from FAR 52.204-21
- **Assessment:** Annual self-assessment. No third-party auditor needed.
- **Cost to implement:** ~$5,000-$15,000 for most small businesses
- **Typical timeline:** 2-4 weeks
- **Who this fits:** Small subcontractors, material suppliers, niche service providers who don't touch CUI

**Key insight for small contractors:** If you're sure you don't handle CUI, Level 1 is straightforward and cheap. But "sure" is a risk — one email with CUI attached can put you in violation.

---

## Level 2 — Advanced (Certified Assessment for Most)

**If you handle CUI.** CUI includes technical data, engineering drawings, source code, security clearances, or any information the government marks as requiring protection.

- **Requirements:** 110 practices aligned with NIST SP 800-171
- **Assessment:** Triennially by a C3PAO (Certified Third-Party Assessment Organization)
- **Cost to implement:** ~$30,000-$100,000 depending on current posture
- **C3PAO assessment cost:** ~$15,000-$30,000
- **Typical timeline:** 6-12 months
- **Who this fits:** Prime contractors, engineering firms, IT service providers, manufacturers

**The trap most small contractors fall into:** They underestimate what Level 2 costs because they focus on the assessment fee ($15-30K) but ignore the implementation cost (policies, tools, training, time).

---

## Level 3 — Expert (Government-Led)

**If you handle the DoD's most sensitive programs.** This is rare for small businesses.

- **Requirements:** All Level 2 practices plus 24 additional practices from NIST SP 800-172
- **Assessment:** Government-led (DIBCAC)
- **Cost:** Significant — typically only relevant for prime contractors on major programs
- **Relevance to small contractors:** Very low. If you need Level 3, you already know.

---

## Which Level Do YOU Need?

Ask one question: **Does your contract require you to handle CUI?**

| If... | You need... |
|-------|-------------|
| Your contract mentions "CUI" or "Controlled Unclassified Information" | Level 2 |
| Your contract says "FAR 52.204-21" but nothing about CUI | Level 1 |
| Your contract is a subcontract and you're not sure | **Ask your prime.** This is the #1 mistake — assuming you don't handle CUI when you actually do |
| You want to win NEW contracts | Plan for Level 2. Most new RFPs require it |

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I self-assess for Level 2?**
A: Only if your contract specifically allows it. Some DoD contracts accept "Level 2 Self-Assessment." Most require a C3PAO assessment. Check your contract's SPRS requirement.

**Q: I'm a subcontractor. Do I need this?**
A: Yes, if your prime contract flows down CMMC requirements. Most do. Ask your prime contracting officer.

**Q: What happens if I'm not certified when my contract renews?**
A: You cannot win or renew DoD contracts without appropriate CMMC certification. No exceptions.

**Q: How long does certification last?**
A: Level 1: 1 year (renew annually). Level 2: 3 years (renew triennially).

**Q: Can I do this myself or do I need a consultant?**
A: Level 1 you can mostly self-implement. Level 2 almost always needs outside help — the 110 practices cover IT, legal, HR, physical security, and supply chain. Most small contractors don't have all that in-house.
